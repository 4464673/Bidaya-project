Did You Know?

A few small facts to get you curious.

Arabic is written right to left, but numbers within Arabic text are written left to right.

Classical Arabic has no capital letters — a letter's shape instead changes depending on where it falls in a word.

Many English words — like "algebra," "coffee," and "cotton" — come from Arabic.

The Arabic script is also used to write other languages, including Persian, Urdu, and historically Ottoman Turkish.
